Privacy policy of the yts-mindblot app

Effective October 17, 2026.

yts-mindblot is a private helper app of the author of the Mindblot YouTube channel. It is intended only for this channel and is used only by its owner.

What data the app accesses

After the channel owner grants consent on the Google consent screen, the app accesses the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API within the requested permissions:

  • uploading videos to the channel and editing the channel's own videos (title, description, status, pinned comment);
  • reading the channel's analytics: views, retention, traffic sources, aggregated audience data.

The app does not access data of other YouTube users beyond the aggregated channel statistics provided by YouTube Analytics.

How the data is used

The data is used only to publish the author's videos on a schedule and to report to the author how the videos perform. It is not sold, not shared with third parties and not used for advertising.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage

The access token is stored encrypted on the author's server. Channel analytics are stored on the same server and are available only to the author.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After revocation the app stops working with the channel; the stored token is deleted together with the channel settings.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the author via the About section of the Mindblot channel on YouTube.
